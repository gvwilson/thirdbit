Closing Time

This workshop is a short introduction to succession planning, disaster management, and ending projects. It assumes that participants have some experience being part of small software or research projects. All of the material is available under an open license, and contributions through our repository are welcome. All contributors are required to respect our Code of Conduct.

Greg Wilson is a programmer, author, and educator based in Toronto. He was the co-founder and first Executive Director of Software Carpentry and received ACM SIGSOFT’s Influential Educator Award in 2020.

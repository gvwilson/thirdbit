Closing Time


Conclusion

Talk About It

  • We created this guide because nothing like it existed — it's incomplete, and we'd like your help
  • Share what you've learned: give talks like [DelBoca2024], post on social media
    • Preferably Mastodon: X and Bluesky are single points of institutional failure
  • Share what hasn't worked so others don't find those landmines the hard way
  • Sharing experiences during difficult times protects your mental health [Pennebaker2016, Cullen2022]

Give Yourself Space

  • Take time to grieve
    • The end of years of work will hurt, even if voluntary
    • Your colleagues may be suffering as well
    • Those who haven't been hit yet may have survivors' guilt
  • People often become altruistic, resourceful, and brave in the wake of disaster [Solnit2010, Yang2024]

Organize

  • Best way to take preventive action: become active in professional associations
    • Unfortunately, meaningful professional associations don't exist in the software industry
  • Membership in voluntary organizations has declined over decades [Putnam2020]
    • We're out of practice — but the bad guys aren't
  • Right now we focus on preserving what we have
    • But playing defense is just a way to lose more slowly
  • See Organizational Change for Open Science for ideas

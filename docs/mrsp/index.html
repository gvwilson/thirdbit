Managing Research Software Projects

This workshop introduces the ideas and tools you need to manage a team of up to a dozen people working together to build research software. Each session ends with something you can take home and use the following week. All of the material is available under an open license, and contributions through our repository are welcome. All contributors are required to respect our Code of Conduct.

Greg Wilson is a programmer, author, and educator based in Toronto. He was the co-founder and first Executive Director of Software Carpentry and received ACM SIGSOFT’s Influential Educator Award in 2020.

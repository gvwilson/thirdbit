Organizational Change

This workshop is a short introduction to organizational change for people with backgrounds in research. All of the material is available under an open license, and contributions through our repository are welcome. All contributors are required to respect our Code of Conduct.

Greg Wilson is a programmer, author, and educator based in Toronto. He was the co-founder and first Executive Director of Software Carpentry and received ACM SIGSOFT’s Influential Educator Award in 2020.

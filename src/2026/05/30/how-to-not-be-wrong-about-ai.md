My post [Twelve Ways to Be Wrong About AI-Assisted Coding][12-ways] seemed to hit a nerve,
so I'm putting together material for a workshop on "How to Not Be Wrong About AI".
As its blurb says:

> This tutorial is a short introduction to research methods you can
> use to figure out whether AI-based programming tools are helping
> your team or not. Even if you don't do such studies yourself, we
> hope that understanding how they work will help you decide which
> claims about them to trust.

It includes a partially-annotated bibliography and further reading;
my thanks to everyone who contributed entries.
If you're interested in taking part in the first run,
please [reach out][email].

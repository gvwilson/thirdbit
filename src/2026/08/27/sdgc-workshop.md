This [one-day workshop][workshop] introduces a few ideas that someone
with a background in computer science (or tech more generally) needs
to know in order to think clearly about the harms of social media and
AI, and about how they should be regulated. I would be very grateful
for [feedback][email]; in particular, I know I'm trying to cram in far
too much, and many of my references are probably out of date.

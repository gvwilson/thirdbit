title: "Dr. Greg Wilson"
---

-   Professional software developer for over 40 years
-   Fellow of the Python Software Foundation
-   Co-founder and first Executive Director of [Software Carpentry](https://carpentries.org).
-   Recipient of ACM SIGSOFT's "Influential Educator of the Year" Award.
-   Co-founder of [It Will Never Work in Theory](https://neverworkintheory.org/) and [The Architecture of Open Source Applications](https://aosabook.org/)
-   Author or editor of fourteen books on programming, one on teaching, and two for children.

<h2 id="employment">Employment</h2>

**2025/10–present**: Independent consulting and teaching.

**2024/04–2025/10**: Engineering Manager, Open Source Libraries, Plotly. Managed in-house  developers; coordinated open source community strategy.

**2021/09–2024/03**: Senior Engineering Manager, Deep Genomics. Recruited and managed  developers; built back-end software in Python; developed and delivered  in-house training

**2021/02–2021/08**: Head of Education, Metabase. Designed and delivered training material;  managed content development team.

**2018/09–2021/01**: Data Scientist and Professional Educator, RStudio PBC. Created and ran  instructor certification program and student intern program.

**2017/08–2018/05**: Content Developer and Instructor Trainer, DataCamp. Created courses  on Git and the Unix shell; recruited and trained freelance instructors.

**2017/01–2017/07**: Principal Consultant, Rangle.io. Revised training materials on Angular  and React; coached company staff on training techniques.

**2015/08–2016/12**: Director of Instructor Training, Software Carpentry Foundation.  Developed and delivered the foundation's train-the-trainers course.

**2012/01–2015/07**: Executive Director, Software Carpentry Foundation. Developed  curriculum; trained instructors; negotiated external partnerships; led  fundraising; led development of workflow tools.

**2011/07-2011/12**: Software Engineer, Side Effects Software Inc. Co-developed online store  for the company's flagship product using Django and Selenium.

**2010/05–2011/06**: Project lead, Software Carpentry. Developed and delivered workshops  on research computing skills; recruited and trained volunteer instructors;  oversaw program assessment and fundraising.

**2007/01–2010/04**: Assistant Professor, Computer Science, University of Toronto. Designed  Professional Master's program; taught courses; supervised theses.

**1982–2006**: Software developer and consultant for academic research centers,  US national labs, and firms ranging from early-stage startups to IBM.

<h2 id="education">Education</h2>

-   **1993**: PhD in Computer Science, University of Edinburgh.
-   **1986**: MSc in Artificial Intelligence, University of Edinburgh.
-   **1984**: BSc in Mathematics and Engineering (First Class Honors), Queen's University.
